Here's something we've learned over more than 10 years: a good industrial floor is mostly decided before any concrete is poured. The loads, the traffic, the joints, the reinforcement and the final finish all work together, so we like to sit down with you at the design stage and get them right on paper. It's far easier than fixing them on site.

We're leaders in the industrial floor market in Jordan, and we bring that experience to every slab. We pour, reinforce and finish to your specification, with the joints planned around your layout.
