Architectural exposed concrete leaves the concrete itself as the finished wall: smooth, with planned panel joints and tie holes. The quality depends on formwork, the mix and care during the pour. This is different from [exposed aggregate](/services/exposed-aggregate), which reveals stone at the surface.
