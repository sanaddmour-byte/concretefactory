# Photo shot list: what the site still needs

The current photos cover stamped concrete very well. Most other services have gaps. The shots below are listed in order of the conversion impact each one unlocks.

**Shoot spec for all shots:**
- Landscape at least 3000 px wide, plus a portrait version where noted.
- Use the golden hour for exteriors and switch the lights on for interiors.
- Clear the site of tape, tools, debris and people's faces unless they have consented.
- Wet the surface for stamped and exposed-aggregate shots so colour and texture show. Hide licence plates.
