# Photo shot list: what the site still needs

Updated after the launch-readiness pass. The site is built only from company-supplied photos. This list says what is already covered, what is held back, and what to shoot next.

**Shoot spec for all shots:**
- Landscape at least 3000 px wide, plus a portrait version where noted (phones show portrait crops).
- Golden hour for exteriors; switch the lights on for interiors.
- Clear the site of tape, tools, debris and people's faces unless they have consented. Hide licence plates and third-party brand names.
- Wet the surface for stamped and exposed-aggregate shots so colour and texture show.
- No filters: colour must stay true (finish swatches are shown unedited).
