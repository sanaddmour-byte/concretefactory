Fire pits & built-in seating

Recessed fire pits and built-in seating for courtyards and gardens.

A recessed concrete lounge sets seating and a fire pit into the ground, creating a gathering place for villas and gardens. Seating, steps and fire pit are finished to match the surrounding surface.

Questions about Fire pits & built-in seating

Is it safe for open flames?

Fire pits need suitable materials and ventilation. We design for the fuel type you choose.

Can it match my paving?

Yes. Seating can be finished to match the stamped or plain concrete around it.

How deep is the lounge?

Depth depends on the site and drainage, and we agree it in the design.

Does it need drainage?

Yes. A recessed area needs drainage planned from the start.
