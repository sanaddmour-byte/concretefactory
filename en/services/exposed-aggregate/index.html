Exposed aggregate

Natural stone revealed at the surface of the slab.

Exposed aggregate removes the thin top layer of cement paste to reveal the stone inside the concrete. The result is a textured, durable surface in the colours of the stone chosen. It is different from architectural exposed concrete walls, which show the fair-faced concrete itself.

  1. What it is

    Exposed aggregate removes the thin top layer of cement paste to reveal the stone inside the concrete. The result is a textured, durable surface in the colours of the stone chosen. It is different from architectural exposed concrete walls, which show the fair-faced concrete itself.

  2. Where it works

    Driveways, Paths and walkways, Pool decks, Courtyards.

  3. Patterns and colours

    Browse our real samples in the Finish Library, such as Exposed aggregate · Daba'a marble pebbles, Terrazzo · Glow-in-the-dark cyan aggregate chips.

  4. How we build it

    Choose the aggregate → Pour and level → Expose the stone → Seal.

  5. Care and lifespan

    Sweep and wash with water and a mild cleaner. A sealer makes cleaning easier.

Questions about Exposed aggregate

Which aggregates can I choose?

Our samples include daba'a marble pebbles and a glow-in-the-dark cyan aggregate. Ask us about other stones.

Is it slippery?

A textured surface generally grips better than a smooth one, but it depends on the stone and sealer.

How is it cleaned?

Sweep and wash with water and a mild cleaner. A sealer makes cleaning easier.

What is the difference from terrazzo?

Terrazzo is ground and polished smooth. Exposed aggregate keeps a textured surface where the stone stands slightly proud.
