Questions we hear most

How long does decorative concrete last?

With a sound sub-base and the right sealer, decorative concrete is a long-lasting surface. Lifespan depends on use, climate and maintenance.

How do I maintain it?

Sweep regularly and wash with a mild cleaner. Avoid harsh acids on sealed surfaces.

When does it need resealing?

Sealers wear with traffic and sun, so exposed outdoor areas are resealed more often than indoor floors.

Will it crack?

Any concrete can develop hairline cracks. Control joints guide where movement happens, and a stamped pattern can hide joints in its lines.

What affects the price?

Area, finish type, thickness and reinforcement, site access, base condition and sealer all affect the price. A site visit gives the most accurate quote.

How long will my project take?

It depends on size, finish and curing time. We confirm the schedule in your quote.

Is it slippery outdoors?

Slip resistance depends on the finish and sealer. Textured stamped finishes and anti-slip sealer additives are options for wet or sloped areas.

Can you pour in Jordan's summer heat?

Hot weather affects setting and curing. Pours are planned for cooler hours and the surface is protected while it cures.
